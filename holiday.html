Every day, the same rhythm

Short bursts you can do on a phone, with something marked at the end of it.

  1. 7:00am · Topic of the day

    A worked example and the rules you must remember, posted in the group and saved in your notes on the site.

Questions students ask

I am going into Form 4 next year. Is this for me?

Yes. It is built for students starting Form 4 in January, and for anyone repeating. We cover the topics that carry the most marks in Paper 1 and Paper 2.

What if I miss a morning?

Everything stays in the group and the notes and quizzes stay on the site, so you catch up the same evening. Only the marked assignment has a same-day deadline.

Do I need data all day?

No. The morning post is a few hundred kilobytes. Install the site once and the notes and quizzes work offline.

Can I pay half now?

Yes, half reserves the place and the rest is due by the first Monday. Message Laban and agree on it before the group fills.

What if I do not like it?

Tell Laban within the first three days and he refunds what you paid, minus nothing. After that the place is yours for the two weeks.
