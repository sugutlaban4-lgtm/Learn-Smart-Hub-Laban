Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

Learn Smart Hub gives students access to notes, past papers, video lessons and quizzes, and offers an optional paid Premium membership. This policy explains what we collect while you use it, why we need it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your full name and email address when you create an account, and a password that is stored in hashed form by our authentication provider. We never see your password.
  • Payment details: if you buy Premium, we receive the M-Pesa number you enter, the amount, the plan chosen, and the M-Pesa confirmation code for the transaction. We do not receive or store your M-Pesa PIN or your M-Pesa balance.
  • Learning activity: which quizzes you attempt and the scores you get, so your dashboard can show your progress.
  • Technical data: the usual server logs kept by our hosting provider, such as the time of a request and any error it caused.

Why we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To activate Premium after a confirmed M-Pesa payment.
  • To show your progress and let you pick up where you stopped.
  • To answer support requests and investigate failed payments.
  • To keep the service working and fix faults.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else is involved

Running the service means sharing the minimum with the providers that make it work:

  • Google Firebase for accounts and for storing your profile and payment records.
  • Safaricom M-Pesa (Daraja) to send you the payment prompt and to confirm the payment.
  • Our hosting providers for the website and the payment service.

These providers process the data on our instructions and under their own security and privacy commitments. We do not pass your details to anyone else unless the law requires it.

Storage on your device

The site keeps a small amount of information in your browser, such as which pages you have opened so they stay readable offline, and your sign-in session. Clearing your browser data removes it. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers.

How long we keep it

Account and payment records are kept while your account exists, because payment records may be needed to resolve a dispute. If you ask us to close your account, we delete your profile and learning activity, and we keep only the payment records we are required to retain.

Your choices

  • Ask for a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong.
  • Ask us to delete your account and the data attached to it.
  • Withdraw from Premium at any time by simply not renewing.

Send any of these requests through the contact form once you are signed in, and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Learn Smart Hub is study material for school students. If you are under 18, please use it with the knowledge of a parent or guardian, and ask them before making any payment.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use the service after that means you accept the updated policy.

Questions about this policy, or want your data removed? Use the contact form while signed in and mention "privacy" in your message.
