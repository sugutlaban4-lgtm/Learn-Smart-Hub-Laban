Refund Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

This policy covers Premium membership bought with M-Pesa on Learn Smart Hub. It is written to be short enough to read before you pay.

When a refund is automatic

  • Payment taken, Premium not activated. If M-Pesa confirms your payment but Premium does not switch on within 30 minutes, tell us and we either activate it or refund the full amount.
  • Charged twice for the same plan. A duplicate payment for the same account and plan is refunded in full.
  • A failed payment that still left your M-Pesa. If the money left your phone but the transaction shows as cancelled or failed, it is usually reversed by Safaricom automatically. If it is not back within 24 hours, send us the M-Pesa code and we will follow it up.

When we refund on request

If something is genuinely wrong with the Premium material, such as a paper or video that does not open, tell us within 7 days of paying. We will try to fix it first, and if we cannot, we refund the amount you paid.

When we do not refund

  • You changed your mind after using Premium material for more than 7 days.
  • The account was suspended for sharing the login or reselling the material.
  • The request relates to a plan that was activated and fully used.
  • You paid the wrong number by mistake and the money never reached us. In that case we will help you trace it, but we cannot refund money we did not receive.

How to ask

Use the contact form while signed in to the account that paid, and include:

  • The M-Pesa confirmation code from the SMS you received.
  • The phone number you paid from.
  • The plan and the date.
  • A short description of the problem.
Refunds are sent back to the same M-Pesa number that paid, usually within 3 to 5 working days after we approve the request. We cannot refund to a different number.

Cancelling

Premium plans here are bought one at a time, not by direct debit, so there is nothing to cancel. When your plan runs out, Premium simply stops and you are charged nothing more.
